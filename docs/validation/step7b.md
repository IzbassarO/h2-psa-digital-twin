## Descriptive summary (hand-written, 2026-10-15; no confirmatory claims)

- **Runs:** 660/660 at CSS (no censored, cap or failed runs). Wall time 4.9 h on 6 workers, 2026-10-14 22:58 to
  2026-10-15 03:51, at commit 464a5f1. Every spot check passed: max |dz| 6.2e-5, 1.6e-5 and 4.9e-5.
- **EP inflow = ED gas** raises z by a median of about 0.55 (about 3.5x the impurity) at every arm's points.
  - P: violates at all 30 points (baseline 17/30), with no feasible point left.
  - G: 2/45 at alpha = 0.05 and 4/45 at alpha = 0.10 (baseline 0/45 and 1/45); coverage 0.96 and 0.91.
  - L: 0/45 at both alphas; coverage 1.00.
  - The G and L hypervolumes are essentially unchanged.
- **Thermal range ends** shift z by about +0.09 (low) and -0.08 (high).
  - Only P changes: 21/30 at the low end, 17/30 at the high end. G and L stay at 0 or 1/45.
  - Recovery at matched productivity moves by +0.35 to +0.71 pp at the low end and up to -0.61 pp at the high end.
- **Reading.** Within this model-form set, the GP-L bounds at n_train = 512 kept every point feasible, and GP-G lost
  a few points under EP = ED gas. Both have large margins at their selected points (Step 6d). The point constraint
  P was the most sensitive. Small samples (30 or 45 points per arm, one training set); descriptive only.
